📺 CrateTube Mac app

For the DJ booth with internet. Load a Serato crate (or just type artist names), get 10 matching songs on YouTube, and download MP3s straight to your MacBook.

What it does

  • Load any .crate — artists are parsed straight from the filenames
  • Or skip the crate and just type artist names
  • 10 YouTube candidates per search with one-tap MP3 download
  • Downloads land in ~/Music/crate-tube, best quality via your proven yt-dlp settings

Setup

  • MacBook with Python 3 and yt-dlp
  • Chrome installed and logged into YouTube (cookies do the work)
