## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & Tips

Even the best apps can hit a snag sometimes. Here are some common questions and quick fixes.

### The app won't open.
Make sure you have downloaded the complete file and that it's not corrupted. Try downloading it again. Also, check that your Windows computer meets the basic requirement of running a 64-bit operating system, which almost all modern computers do.

### The app is not showing my playlists.
First, make sure you're logged in with the correct Spotify account. Then, try logging out and logging back in from within the app. A quick restart of the app usually fixes this too.

### The sound is stuttering or cutting out.
This is usually a network issue. Check your Wi-Fi or ethernet connection. Since SpotLight is so light, it shouldn't be a problem with your computer's performance.

### I don't have a Spotify Premium account. Can I still use it?
Yes, you can use SpotLight with a free Spotify account. However, you may experience ads and have limited control over which specific songs you play, just like with the official app. For the best experience, a Premium account is recommended.

### Is my password safe?
**Absolutely.** SpotLight never sees or stores your Spotify password. The login happens securely through Spotify's own website, and SpotLight only receives a special, revocable "key" to control your playback.

### How do I update SpotLight?
We recommend checking the same download link you used before for new versions. When a new version is released, just download the new file and run it. Your settings and login information will be saved.
